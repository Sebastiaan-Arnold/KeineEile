Common questions

What do the colours mean?

Green "leave in 5 min": you have time. Orange "leave now": start walking. Red "Eile!": you'll only make it if you hurry.

Why does the app need my location?

To find the stops near you and work out how long the walk is. Without it, the app can't tell you when to leave. See the privacy policy.

The walking time doesn't fit me.

Go to Settings → Getting to the stop. "Learn my walking pace" adjusts the times to how fast you walk, and "Extra time" adds minutes, for example to get down from your flat.

A departure was wrong or late.

Departures come from Wiener Linien's live data and can be late, incomplete or wrong. Keine Eile shows what Wiener Linien publish. Allow some extra time when it matters.

My widget doesn't update every minute.

iOS decides how often widgets may refresh, usually every 5 to 15 minutes. The widget counts down in between. Opening the app refreshes everything immediately.

The way to my destination says "No Wiener Linien connection nearby".

Keine Eile only covers Wiener Linien in Vienna. Outside the network there is no connection to plan.

Is this an official Wiener Linien app?

No. Keine Eile is an independent app using Wiener Linien open data (CC BY 4.0).
