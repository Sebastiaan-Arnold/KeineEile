Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

In short: Keine Eile has no account, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. There is no server of our own, so we never receive your data. Your favourites, destination and settings stay on your iPhone. Your location is only shared with Apple and Wiener Linien when a feature you use needs it.

Who is responsible

Sebastiaan Arnold, the developer of Keine Eile. For questions about your data or this policy: privacy@keineeile.app.

1. Data that stays on your iPhone

The app keeps the following only on your device, in its own storage, which it shares with its widgets:

  • your favourite lines and the order you put them in
  • your destination (its name, icon and address) and your route preference
  • your extra time and the places you added for it
  • your settings, for example which vehicles to show
  • a learned walking-pace factor, if you turned that on
  • a copy of the Wiener Linien stop lists

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It stays until you change or delete it in the app, or delete the app.

2. Location and step data

Your location. With your permission, the app uses your location while it is open, to find the stops near you, work out the walk and plan the way to your destination. If you allow it in iOS, its widgets and Siri answers use it too. The app never tracks you in the background. It keeps only the last position it saw, on your iPhone, so widgets and Siri still work when your iPhone can't provide one.

Step data (optional). If you turn on "Learn my walking pace", the app reads the step data on your iPhone from the last 7 days to learn how fast you walk. This happens entirely on your iPhone. The step data is used once and not stored; only the resulting factor is kept. Turning the setting off deletes the factor. You can also withdraw the Motion & Fitness permission at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

Without these permissions you can still search lines and stations and see departures. Walking times, nearby stops and the way to your destination need your location.

3. What is sent to other services

Only when a feature you use needs it:

  • Apple Maps: your position and the position of nearby stops, to calculate walking times. When you search for an address, what you type. When you choose "Use my current location", your position, to find the street name.
  • Wiener Linien: if you set a destination, your current position and your destination's position go to the Wiener Linien journey planner to find the fastest way there. To show departures, the app asks Wiener Linien for the departures of specific stops; those requests contain stop numbers, not your position.

Apple and Wiener Linien handle these requests under their own privacy policies: Apple · Wiener Linien. Nothing is sent anywhere else. The app contains no third-party code, advertising or analytics.

Purposes and legal basis

  • Location, for nearby stops, walking times and the way to your destination: to provide the features you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The app only gets your location if you allow it in iOS.
  • Step data, for your walking pace: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR insofar as step data counts as health data), which you give by turning the feature on and can withdraw at any time by turning it off.
  • Data on your iPhone, such as favourites and settings: to provide the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • This website: see below (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the service secure; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a working, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The website itself uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the app stores your data only on your iPhone, you can view, change or delete it yourself in the app, or by deleting the app. If you have a question, contact us (privacy@keineeile.app). You can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority (Datenschutzbehörde).

Children

Keine Eile collects no data about anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
