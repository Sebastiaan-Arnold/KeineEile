About Keine Eile

"Keine Eile" is German for "no rush".

You're about to leave the flat, the U-Bahn goes in six minutes, and the stop is a five-minute walk away. Can you still make it, or do you have to run? Departure boards tell you when the train leaves, but not when you need to leave.

Keine Eile answers that one question. It combines the live departures of Wiener Linien with your walk to the stop and shows, at a glance, whether you have time, should leave now, or only make it if you hurry.

A hobby project

Keine Eile is a hobby project by Sebastiaan Arnold, made in spare time. There's no company behind it and no business plan. The goal isn't to make money, but to make something useful: a small tool that makes everyday trips around Vienna a little calmer.

That's why Keine Eile is free, and it's meant to stay that way.

What makes it different

It tells you when to leaveNot just when the train goes, but when you need to walk out of the door to catch it.
Your walk, your paceWalking times from Apple Maps, adjusted to how fast you really walk, plus extra time if you need it.
One glance is enoughGreen, orange or red: in the app, in widgets, on the Lock Screen and through Siri.

What I care about

Calm, not hurryThe name says it: a good tool lets you stop checking and just go.
PrivacyNo tracking, no analytics and no server of my own. Your favourites and settings stay on your iPhone, and there's nothing to sell. Read the privacy policy.
HonestyThe app shows what Wiener Linien publish, and tells you when times might be out of date instead of pretending they're fresh.
SimplicityIt does one thing and tries to do it well. No clutter, no feeds, nothing that doesn't help you get out of the door on time.

Where the data comes from

  • Departures, disruptions and journeys: Wiener Linien open data, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Times can be late, incomplete or wrong, so allow some extra time when it matters.
  • Walking times and address search: Apple Maps.
