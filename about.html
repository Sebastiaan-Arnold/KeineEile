About Keine Eile

"Keine Eile" is German for "no rush".

You're about to leave the flat, the U-Bahn goes in six minutes, and the stop is a five-minute walk away. Can you still make it, or do you have to run? Departure boards tell you when the train leaves, but not when you need to leave.

Keine Eile answers that one question. It combines the live departures of Wiener Linien with your walk to the stop and shows, at a glance, whether you have time, should leave now, or only make it if you hurry.

A hobby project

Keine Eile is a hobby project by Sebastiaan Arnold, made in spare time. There's no company behind it and no business plan. The goal isn't to make money, but to make something useful: a small tool that makes everyday trips around Vienna a little calmer.

That's why Keine Eile is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription and no account. It's meant to stay that way.

What makes it different

  • It tells you when to leave. Not just when the train goes, but when you need to walk out of the door to catch it.
  • Your walk, your pace. Walking times come from Apple Maps, and the app can learn how fast you really walk. Need a few extra minutes to get down to the street? You can add them.
  • One glance is enough. Green, orange or red, on the main screen, in widgets, on the Lock Screen and through Siri. No timetables to read, no maths in your head.

What I care about

  • Calm, not hurry. The name says it: a good tool lets you stop checking and just go.
  • Privacy. No tracking, no analytics and no server of my own. Your favourites and settings stay on your iPhone, and there's nothing to sell. Read the privacy policy.
  • Honesty. The app shows what Wiener Linien publish, and it tells you when the times might be out of date instead of pretending they're fresh.
  • Simplicity. Keine Eile does one thing and tries to do it well. No clutter, no feeds, nothing that doesn't help you get out of the door on time.

Where the data comes from

  • Departures, disruptions and journeys: Wiener Linien open data, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Times can be late, incomplete or wrong, so allow some extra time when it matters.
  • Walking times and address search: Apple Maps.
